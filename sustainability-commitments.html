Sustainability

How Family Bank brings environmental, social and governance thinking into the way we lend, operate and serve customers.

Our Approach to Sustainability

Sustainability at Family Bank means growing responsibly: lending in ways that support people and the environment, running our operations with less waste, and holding ourselves to clear standards.

Our ESG Commitments

Supporting customers and businesses that care for the environment through responsible lending.

Giving more people access to flexible, affordable financial services.

Running the bank with transparency, accountability and sound risk management.

Understanding and managing climate-related risks in our business and our lending.

Building a workplace and partnerships that benefit our staff and the communities we serve.

How We Put Sustainability Into Practice

Three areas show how sustainability shapes our banking: responsible banking principles, green lending and the way we run our operations.

Responsible Banking

Six principles guide how we align our business with the SDGs and the Paris Agreement.

  • Alignment: We align our strategy with the needs of people and the goals society has set for a sustainable future.
  • Impact & Target Setting: We work to understand our impact and set targets where we can make the most difference.
  • Clients & Customers: We work with customers to encourage responsible practices and shared prosperity.
  • Stakeholders: We consult and partner with the people and groups who have a stake in our work.
  • Governance & Culture: We build responsible banking into how we lead and how we work.
  • Transparency & Accountability: We report on our progress openly and honestly.

Green Agribusiness Financing

Lending that supports farmers and agribusinesses and rewards practices that protect the land.

  • Financing for farmers and agribusiness SMEs, from tea, coffee and dairy to grain, fish and sugar.
  • Lending that encourages practices that protect the land and water.
  • Products for equipment and inputs that cut waste and save energy.

Digital & Operational

The first to introduce paperless banking through smart card technology, so customers transact without deposit or withdrawal slips.

  • Paperless banking pioneer: the first to introduce paperless banking through smart card technology (from the Company Profile).
  • Low-bandwidth mobile banking: our PesaPap mobile app is built to work well on low bandwidth, so customers with weaker connections can still bank.

Contributing to the Sustainable Development Goals

We align our work with 13 of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals set by the United Nations.
