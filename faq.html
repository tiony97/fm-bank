You can open an account from your phone or at a branch.

On your phone (PesaPap): Download the PesaPap app, or dial *325# and choose "Open Account". Pick your account type, accept the terms and fill in your details. Take a selfie, then upload a clear photo of your ID (front and back) and your signature. Review your application, then deposit cash to activate the account, or save it for 30 days if you don't have cash yet. You'll get a one-time password, then reset your PIN and set your security questions.

What to have ready: your original ID, your KRA PIN, your mobile number, KES 100 and a smartphone

At a branch: Bring a photocopy of your national ID or valid passport, any amount of cash to open the account and a passport photo. Business accounts also need KRA PIN certificates [Confirm the documents required on the individual account's page].

Family Bank customers use Visa debit and credit cards to access their money at ATMs worldwide and pay at merchants. The main options are:

  • Debit card: linked to your account, for ATM withdrawals and purchases.
  • Credit card: available as Classic and Gold, with online purchases allowed and up to 50 interest-free days
  • Prepaid card: open to all Family Bank customers, and designed to be safer than carrying cash
  • Virtual Cards

Eligibility and documents differ by card. A credit card application typically needs a signed form, ID, KRA PIN, recent pay slip and employer letter.

Queen Banking is Family Bank's proposition for women, offering both financial and non-financial support. It launched on 22 September 2023 and serves women in business, professional women, chama women and Diaspora Queens, with a focus on the woman and her community.

What do you get:

  • Financial: transactional and savings accounts, payment solutions, secured and unsecured credit, chama savings and investment options, and tailor-made insurance
  • Beyond money: financial education, recognition and wellness, and networking.

Who counts as a "woman in business"? A business at least 51% owned by women, a business where women hold over 30% of the board or over 25% of senior management, or one with products or services built for women

Explore Queen Banking Join Queen Banking

To join, visit any Family Bank branch or contact our Queen Banking team. You will need your ID, KRA PIN, and to complete a short application form.

Queen Banking offers tailored current and savings accounts, as well as personal and business loans with favorable terms for women.

Your business qualifies if it is at least 51% owned by women, or if women hold over 30% of the board or over 25% of senior management.

Members gain access to exclusive financial education webinars, networking events, mentorship programs, and wellness initiatives designed for women.

Yes, chama groups and women in the diaspora are welcome to join and can access tailored savings and investment products.
