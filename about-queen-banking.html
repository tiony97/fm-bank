About Queen Banking

Family Bank is dedicated to empowering women in the world of finance. We understand the unique challenges women face, whether you are a business owner, a professional or a group of women seeking financial growth, and we are committed to giving you the support you need to thrive.

Since 2023, we have recognised the vital role women play in the economy. Through specialised products and services, we address the challenges women entrepreneurs and professionals face and give them the resources and opportunities they need.

Queen Banking is also a community where women connect, share experiences and support one another, with networking, capacity building and essential resources. With our strategic partners, we share education and information with women entrepreneurs, recognise their contributions and open up avenues for growth and investment. Queen Banking is Family Bank's commitment to uplift women in Kenya, as key drivers of economic development and societal change.
