Credit Cards

Life doesn't wait, and neither do we, the bank that grows with you, from your first pay slip to your family's future. #WithYouForLife

Credit That Works When You Do

A Family Bank credit card gives you a credit limit to spend from, so you can cover larger purchases or bridge a tight month. Pay within the interest-free period and you pay no interest, or repay over time when you need the flexibility.

Benefits

  • No joining fee
  • Repay in full or in installments
  • Up to 50 interest-free days
  • Visa Classic and Visa Gold available
  • Cardholder insured against death or permanent disability, with free lost card cover
  • Spend locally and abroad, in store and online

What we need from you

  • National ID and KRA PIN certificate
  • Three recent pay slips or proof of income
  • Bank statements

Know What You'll Pay

Clear, upfront fees on this card, so there are no surprises.

  • Joining fee - Waived.
  • Annual fee - Kes 3,000 (Classic), Kes 4,000 (Gold).
  • Monthly interest - 3.5% per month.
  • Cash advance fee - 6.5% of amount accessed, minimum Kes 200.
  • Late payment fee - 6%, minimum Kes 200.
  • Over limit fee - 4% of the excess, minimum Kes 1,500.
  • Card replacement fee - Kes 1,000.
  • Supplementary card annual fee - Kes 1,000 (Classic), Kes 2,000 (Gold).
  • Minimum repayment - 20% of outstanding balance.
  • Insurance fee - 0.4% per annum of the credit limit.

**Fees are subject to prevailing withholding tax and other taxes as may be applicable.

**Terms and Conditions Apply.
