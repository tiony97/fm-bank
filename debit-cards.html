Debit Cards

Your money, wherever you are, Mastercard and Visa debit cards linked to your account.

Spend Straight From Your Account

Your debit card gives you direct access to your money, with no borrowing involved. Withdraw cash at ATMs, pay at shops and merchants, and check your balance, all linked to your Family Bank account.

Benefits

  • Withdraw cash at ATMs and pay at merchants
  • Pay online and for e-commerce
  • Accepted at Visa and Mastercard outlets locally and abroad
  • Mastercard World Debit with airport lounge access on Fuzu accounts

What we need from you

  • An active Family Bank account

Know What You'll Pay

Clear, upfront fees on this card, so there are no surprises.

  • Visa Debit Classic / Mastercard Gold Debit issuance - Kes 500.
  • Visa Debit Classic / Mastercard Gold Debit replacement - Kes 500.
  • Visa Debit Classic / Mastercard Gold Debit supplementary card issuance - Kes 500.
  • Visa Debit Classic / Mastercard Gold Debit supplementary card replacement - Kes 500.
  • Visa Debit Gold / Mastercard World Debit issuance - Kes 750.
  • Visa Debit Gold / Mastercard World Debit replacement - Kes 750.
  • Visa Debit Gold / Mastercard World Debit supplementary card issuance - Kes 750.
  • Visa Debit Gold / Mastercard World Debit supplementary card replacement - Kes 750.
  • Annual fee - As may be advised from time to time.
  • Family Bank ATM cash withdrawal - Kes 30.
  • Balance enquiry - Kes 10.
  • Mini statement - Kes 10.
  • Cash deposit (counter and ATMs) - Nil.
  • POS transactions - Nil.
  • Pesapoint ATM withdrawal - Kes 80.
  • Kenswitch ATM withdrawal - Kes 100.
  • Local Visa/Mastercard ATM withdrawal - Kes 200.
  • International Visa/Mastercard ATM withdrawal - Kes 200.
  • Retrieval of captured card at Family Bank ATMs - Free.

**Fees are subject to prevailing withholding tax and other taxes as may be applicable.

**Terms and Conditions Apply.
