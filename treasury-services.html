Treasury Services

Foreign exchange, money market services and market information from our treasury team.

Foreign Exchange Services

Buy, sell and manage foreign currency with our treasury team – from everyday spot deals to accounts, cheques and loans in foreign currency.

Foreign Exchange Spot

Buy or sell foreign currency for delivery within 2 business days. We offer very competitive rates and same-day value on telegraphic transfers in USD, GBP, EUR, AED, AUD, ZAR, CAD, JPY, UGX, TZS, RWF and other currencies.

Forward Contracts

An agreement to exchange two currencies at a set time in the future, after the spot date (beyond 2 days). Available for all major currencies at competitive rates, for customers and non-customers.

Currency Swaps

An agreement between two parties to exchange principal and/or interest payments in one currency for payments of equal value in another. Family Bank offers competitive pricing on the swap points.

Foreign Currency Banker's Cheques (Local)

Banker's cheques in USD, GBP and Euro for local banks.

Structured Deals

Tailored structured finance solutions from Treasury, Trade Finance and Corporate Banking.

Foreign Current Accounts

Current or savings accounts in USD, GBP and Euro.

Foreign Currency Lending

Foreign currency loans can be used to finance both individual and corporates.

Money Market Services

Place and grow your funds with our treasury team. Use the calculator to see what a placement could earn before you speak to us.

Call & Fixed Deposits

We negotiate favorable rates on local and foreign currencies placement for call or fixed periods to give superior returns for our esteemed clients. The tenor ranges from 1 month to 1 year for fixed deposits (specified period), with our rates being very competitive.

Treasury Bills & Bonds

Government securities are debt instruments issued by the government. Family Bank buys them from the Central Bank of Kenya on your behalf, guides you through applying, follows up until payment is done and shares the auction results as soon as they are available.
