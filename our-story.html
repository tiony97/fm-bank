Our Story

Since 1984, we have worked to positively transform people's lives through quality financial services.

Who We Are

Family Bank was established in 1984 as Family Bank Building Society with just one branch. It has since grown and became a fully-fledged commercial bank in May 2007. The Bank is regulated by the Central Bank of Kenya (CBK).

Family Bank has a strong retail customer base, focuses on SME banking and has a vision to positively transform people's lives in Africa.

The Bank is renowned for its digital innovation, being the first to introduce paperless banking through smart card technology. This allows customers to transact without filling in deposit or withdrawal slips. Family Bank is also a pioneer in mobile banking with its PesaPap mobile app, which is unparalleled in the market.

Family Bank provides retail and consumer products, SME, agribusiness, corporate banking, trade finance, and insurance products. In line with its county banking roadmap, the Bank is leveraging the country’s devolved governance system to attract more customers. This strategy aims to utilise the opportunities the devolved governance structure offers, positioning Family Bank as a one-stop-shop for all banking needs

Years of Service

Our Journey Since 1984

Family Bank began in 1984 with one branch and a clear vision: to be the institution of choice for people the mainstream banks had ignored. Follow the milestones that have shaped us, from our first customers to the bank we are today, and the people and purpose that have carried us.

1984

Family Finance Building Society was started in 1984 to be the institution of choice for the unbanked, serving the mass market the mainstream banks ignored: small businesses, jua kali artisans, farmers, teachers, junior government employees and NGOs.

Our Mission

Our mission has been to liberate the unbanked from financial bondage and the cycle of poverty, without discrimination as to creed, race, age or gender.

Today

Today, Family Bank is a top tier bank with over 90 branches and an extensive digital network. We are listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange, serving millions of customers across the region.

As We Grew

As we grew, we expanded our services to include microfinance, SME banking, and digital banking solutions. We established partnerships that allowed us to reach more communities.

Our People

Our people have been the cornerstone of our success. Through continuous training and a customer-first culture, we have built a team that is passionate about transforming lives.

The Road Ahead

The future is filled with endless possibilities. We remain committed to empowering our customers and driving sustainable growth in the communities we serve.
