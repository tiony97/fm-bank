SECURITY OPERATIONS CENTRE (SOC) ANALYST

December 3, 2026

SOC/10/2026

REPORTING TO: HEAD OF INFORMATION SECURITY

Job Purpose:

The SOC Analyst is responsible for continuous 24/7 monitoring, detection, analysis, and response to cybersecurity events across the bank's technology environment. The role safeguards the bank's information systems and data by identifying and responding to cyber threats, performing real-time monitoring, incident investigation, proactive threat hunting, and disciplined incident response, ensuring timely threat containment, regulatory compliance, and the uninterrupted delivery of customer-facing services.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Monitor security alerts across security tools such as SIEM, EDR/XDR, NDR, DLP/S, WAF, DLP, email security, and cloud security platforms.
  • Triage, analyse, and classify security events based on severity and impact.
  • Investigate suspicious activity and escalate incidents in line with the Incident Response Plan.
  • Undertake triage, correlation, and analysis of security events, and escalate critical incidents to the Incident Response Team.
  • Support incident investigation, containment, eradication, and recovery activities for security incidents.
  • Preserve digital evidence in line with forensic and chain-of-custody standards.
  • Operate and maintain SIEM, EDR/XDR, and other security monitoring tools.
  • Support the implementation of automation playbooks to enhance SOC operations.
  • Review threat intelligence feeds relevant to the banking sector.
  • Assess applicability of global threat alerts to the bank's environment.
  • Contribute to SOC KPIs and KRIs aligned to operational performance metrics.

Qualification & Experience:

  • Bachelor's degree in IT, Computer Science, Cybersecurity, or related field.
  • Professional certification such as CISA, CISM, CEH, CompTIA Security+, CSA, OSCP, or relevant security certification.
  • Minimum of 2-3 years in SOC or cybersecurity operations (L1-L2 levels).
  • Experience with SIEM, EDR/XDR, network security, firewall and endpoint logs.
  • Experience in banking or regulated environments is an advantage.

Key Competencies:

  • 1–3 years in SOC or cybersecurity operations (covers L1–L2 levels)
  • Experience with SIEM, EDR/XDR, network security, firewall and endpoint logs.
  • Experience in banking or regulated environments is an advantage.

ALL applicants MUST apply online to: recruitment@familybank.co.ke quoting the reference number. Closing date is 03rd November 2026. Canvassing will automatically disqualify the candidate. Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.
